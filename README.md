# news-image-20261125 — Wednesday 25 November 2026 in one picture

A newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 25 November 2026 in a
single 1024×1024 image: 34 stories, all dated to that day (UTC).

## What the picture shows

- **3 large illustrated hero panels** — the Shenzhou-24 launch carrying
  the first Pakistani into orbit, the overnight Russian strikes on Odesa
  and Kyiv, and the Asia tech rout (Nikkei −2.3%, Kospi −4.8%).
- **A UTC timeline** of the day as reported, from the 00:18 Shenzhou-24
  liftoff to the evening Trump–Rutte meeting.
- **"THE DAY IN NUMBERS"** — 6 big statistics of the day.
- **10 numbered panels** with a small illustration, a region and topic
  tag, a headline, a short deck, and a source-status chip
  (CONFIRMED / REPORTED) — from the Villavicencio car bomb to the
  Pacific's PacGuard maritime watch system.
- **"Where it happened"** — a world map with numbered pins and a
  per-region story count.
- **"Also on Wednesday 25 November"** — 21 more verified stories in
  brief, numbered 14–34.
- **A DATE CHECK strip** listing the big stories deliberately left out
  because they broke before 25 November (COP31, the Huawei verdict, the
  CIA/ODNI hack, the Dow crossing 50,000) and the ones still to come
  (the US PCE print at 13:30 UTC, Thanksgiving, COP32).

Every story was cross-checked against its wire dateline, not an
aggregator timestamp: several stories that looked like 25 November news
were in fact from 23–24 November and were moved to the DATE CHECK box.
One claim (a magnitude 6.2 Dodecanese quake) was left out entirely after
verification showed the real event was a smaller 24 November tremor.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
# writes /tmp/out/entry.png (1024x1024 PNG, under 1 MiB)
```

`main.py` uses only the Python standard library. It copies the poster
`entry.png` that ships in this repository to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` and
verifies the PNG header is 1024×1024 first.

## How it was made

The poster was drawn in the build session by a fresh Python/Pillow
generator (not shipped in this repository) from a story list researched
that same day. The generator, the story list and the render checks live
outside the repo; what ships here is only the finished image and the
copy program above.
